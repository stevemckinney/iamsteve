Andy Clarke published a new book recently ‘Standout designs for the web’. I’ve always enjoyed Andy’s writing and design style. It’s been a while since I’ve read a book by Andy. I can’t quite believe it was 2010’s Hardboiled Web Design, which I still have a copy of. I feel like I’ve read more recent things, but maybe not.

## I want the book to challenge me
I had been following a few of the announcements around this new book and decided to pre-order. Andy’s push for art direction on the web and the character he brings with his designs is something I believe he’s know for. It’s something I aspire to do myself, albeit I do find it challenging. 

But what’s drawn me to the book? I guess my career path has diverged away from this more expressive approach. I’ve been acknowledging that lately, as I’ve been feeling a bit uninspired. Being familiar with Andy’s style is one that challenges the standard.

Creativity is a muscle that you need to train and this book felt like it has been placed in front of me at the right time.



There’s a simple solution, I need to be creatively challenging myself more frequently.
